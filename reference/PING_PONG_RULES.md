# Phase (phase4o4) Ping-Pong and Classic rules (from 6 reels, 7 Oct 2026)
Liquidity to mark: the Asia high and low (20:00-00:00 NY), the London high and low, and 15m/1H buyside (swing highs) and sellside (swing lows).
## Classic (with the trend)
1. 1H/15m trend, set by BOS/shift.
2. Fib on the HTF impulse leg. Wait for price to pull back into the HTF OTE (0.618-0.786) AND sweep liquidity (Asia high/low, buyside or sellside).
3. On the 5m/1m, wait for a shift back in the trend direction. Draw a fib on that leg and set a limit order at the 0.618-0.786 OTE.
4. Stop beyond the 1.0 (the swing extreme). Target the opposing HTF liquidity. Take partials along the way.
## Ping-Pong (range)
1. After a sweep of a marked pool, look for an LTF reversal confirmation: a shift then OTE, an IFVG, an order block after the shift, or a Unicorn (breaker plus FVG).
2. Stop beyond the sweep extreme. Target the opposite pool.
3. When the opposite pool is swept, look for the reverse trade back the other way.
4. After a valid loss, wait for a fresh confirmation and try once more.
